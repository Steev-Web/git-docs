Yes, they can coexist, but there are some important DNS rules.

If you're using:

Resend for transactional emails (OTP, password resets, receipts)
Brevo for newsletters

that's a common setup.

They won't affect each other if:
Their DKIM records use different selectors (they usually do).
Their tracking CNAMEs use different hostnames.
You don't create conflicting records with the same host and type.
The main thing to watch out for is SPF

Both services typically ask you to add an SPF record. You cannot have two separate SPF TXT records for the same domain. For example, this is incorrect:

TXT @ "v=spf1 include:spf.resend.com ~all"
TXT @ "v=spf1 include:spf.brevo.com ~all"

Instead, you combine them into one SPF record, for example:

v=spf1 include:spf.resend.com include:spf.brevo.com ~all

(Use the exact includes provided by each service's documentation.)

Recommendation
✅ Use Resend for transactional emails.
✅ Use Brevo for newsletters.
✅ Combine the SPF records into one if both require SPF.
✅ Add both providers' DKIM records.
